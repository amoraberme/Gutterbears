## Meta Ads Manager Upload & Deployment Checklist

When publishing these ads into Meta Ads Manager (`adsmanager.facebook.com`):
1. **Never Click "Boost Post"** (Masterclass Page 10): Avoid the 30% Apple Service Fee and access full Conversions API / Advantage+ placements.
2. **Select Creatives Directly from Folder:** Open the target Ad Set folder, pick the PNG asset, and paste the corresponding Primary Text, Headline, Description, and CTA from its `ad_set_captions.md`.
3. **Configure ManyChat Payloads:** Set the "Send Message" button trigger payload to match the `ManyChat Trigger` specified in the caption file.
4. **Enforce 15%–20% Scaling Rule:** Review weekly metrics (CPM $\le$ ₱180, CTR $\ge$ 1.8%, Cost/Msg $\le$ ₱95). Scale winning Ad Sets by 15%–20% every 48 to 72 hours.
5. **Philippine 12% VAT:** Ensure TIN and Muntinlupa business address are registered in Payment Settings for official BIR deductible invoices.
